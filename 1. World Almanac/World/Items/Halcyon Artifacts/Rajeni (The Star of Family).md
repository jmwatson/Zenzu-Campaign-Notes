**Armor**: Gloves
Bonus Stats:
	+1 AC
	+1 Saving Throws
	+1 Attack Rolls
**Weight**: 0.5 lb.
> [!example]+ Special Abilities
> **Form Bond (Action)**:
> - Form a bond with a target player until next long rest or ability is used again. (*2 / Long Rest*)
> **Heroism (Action)**:
> - (*2 / Long Rest*)

> [!example]+ Bonded Bonuses
> **Stability**:
> - When bonded target is reduced to 0hp, they are stabilized and do not need to make saving throws. Also receives effects of Sanctuary until they are healed. (*1/Long Rest*)
> **Retribution**:
> - When bonded target is damaged by an enemy, they enemy makes a Wisdom saving throw. (*1/Round*)
> 	- On fail, enemy takes 3d6 psychic damage and half that on success
> **Attack**:
> - When bonded target hits an opponent within your melee range, you may make a melee attack on that same opponent (*1/Round*)
> **Restoration**:
> - Bonded target receives the effects of a Greater Restoration. (*1/LongRest*)
